# Video Storyboard

See the root `VIDEO_STORYBOARD.md` for the full promo storyboard. The MVP demo sequence is:

1. Owner logs in and opens Otieno Family Memories.
2. Contributor uploads a photo and writes a story.
3. Approver reviews the pending memory.
4. Approved memories generate Family Highlights.
5. Family Highlights opens in flip-album mode on desktop and mobile.
