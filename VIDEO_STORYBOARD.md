# Memory Circle MVP Promo Video Storyboard

## Video purpose

Create a short 60-90 second promotional explainer video for Memory Circle. The video should introduce the problem, show the solution, and make the MVP easy to understand for developers, early testers, and potential users.

## Target audience

- Families spread across countries
- Diaspora communities
- Alumni groups
- Schools/churches/community groups
- Early software testers
- Potential investors or partners

## Core message

Your photos can stay where they are. Memory Circle turns them into a shared, approved, beautiful flip album that your family or community can build together.

## Video structure

### Scene 1: Scattered memories

Duration: 7 seconds

Visual:

- Phone gallery
- PC folder
- Google Drive icon placeholder
- iCloud icon placeholder
- OneDrive icon placeholder
- Photos scattered across screen

Voiceover:

"Our memories are everywhere — on phones, laptops, cloud drives, and old family computers."

On-screen text:

"Photos everywhere. Stories scattered."

### Scene 2: The problem

Duration: 8 seconds

Visual:

- Family members in different locations
- Messages saying "Send me the photos"
- Duplicate albums
- Grandparent looking at an empty frame or old photo

Voiceover:

"But sharing them as real family stories is still difficult. Photos get lost, duplicated, or buried in endless galleries."

On-screen text:

"Sharing is easy. Curating memories is hard."

### Scene 3: Introducing Memory Circle

Duration: 8 seconds

Visual:

- Memory Circle logo placeholder
- Circle animation connecting family members
- Shared album appears at center

Voiceover:

"Memory Circle creates a private shared space where families and communities build living memory albums together."

On-screen text:

"Introducing Memory Circle"

### Scene 4: Keep photos where they are

Duration: 9 seconds

Visual:

- User selects source: Phone, PC, Google Drive, iCloud, OneDrive
- Photos flow into album as references/cache, not full migration

Voiceover:

"Your photos can stay where you already keep them — on your device, PC, Google Drive, iCloud, OneDrive, or external archive."

On-screen text:

"No forced migration. Connect what you already have."

### Scene 5: Contribute memories

Duration: 8 seconds

Visual:

- User uploads/selects photo
- Adds caption
- Adds story
- Submits for approval

Voiceover:

"Members add photos, captions, dates, events, and stories from any device."

On-screen text:

"Add photos + stories"

### Scene 6: Approve and curate

Duration: 8 seconds

Visual:

- Approver sees pending memory
- Buttons: Approve, Edit & Approve, Request Changes, Reject

Voiceover:

"Trusted members approve, edit, and curate what becomes part of the official album."

On-screen text:

"Trusted curation. Clean family albums."

### Scene 7: Flip album display

Duration: 12 seconds

Visual:

- Desktop full-screen album
- Tap/click page flip
- Mobile swipe flip
- Two-page desktop spread
- Single-page phone layout

Voiceover:

"Approved memories appear in a beautiful tap-to-flip album — on PCs, phones, tablets, and living-room screens."

On-screen text:

"A classic album, rebuilt for connected families."

### Scene 8: Archive confidence

Duration: 8 seconds

Visual:

- Album health screen
- "2 devices have full archive"
- "Display copies available offline"
- "Cloud backup optional"

Voiceover:

"Memory Circle keeps the album organized, cached, and ready for future archive protection."

On-screen text:

"Designed for long-term memory protection"

### Scene 9: Closing

Duration: 7 seconds

Visual:

- Family album flipping slowly
- Members connected around the album
- Logo and call to action

Voiceover:

"Memory Circle: everyone contributes, trusted people curate, and the album lives everywhere."

On-screen text:

"Memory Circle\nEveryone contributes. Trusted people curate. The album lives everywhere."

## Visual style

- Warm, calm, family-oriented
- Paper texture backgrounds
- Soft shadows
- Modern but nostalgic
- Avoid overly technical visuals
- Use rounded UI cards
- Use gentle page-turn animation

## Music

- Soft piano or acoustic background
- Gentle, optimistic, reflective

## Deliverables

- 60-90 second MP4
- 16:9 landscape version
- 9:16 mobile/social version later
- Still thumbnail image
- Optional silent version with subtitles
